### Added

- **Suggestions through the API.** A reviewer sends a draft of a component as a suggestion, with no lock, while its author goes on editing. Whoever may edit the component accepts it, applied to the component as it is now and recorded as their version, or rejects it; its author may withdraw it. One that no longer fits says where. Every pending suggestion on a component or a document, or one person's, can be accepted or rejected at once.
