### Changed

- **Duplicate, redrawn.** A document's copy starts from one of three choices, shown as cards, then lets you set any one component to Copy or Reuse from a folding list. A component you may not read is now reused, unnamed, rather than stopping the copy.

### Added

- **Keep or leave local rewordings.** Duplicating a document that has local rewordings asks whether the copy keeps them or shows each component's own words.
