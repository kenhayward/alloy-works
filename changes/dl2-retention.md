### Added

- **Deleted, beside Archived.** Each listing's Deleted filter lists the deleted components or documents you may read, with who deleted each and when, and Undelete for whoever may. Undeleting is never refused.
- **Retention.** Administration > Retention sets the least number of days a component or a document is kept before it may be deleted, for a kind, a space or both; the longest applies. Delete says a keep refuses it, and until when.
