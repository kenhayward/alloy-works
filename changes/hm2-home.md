### Added

- **Needs your attention, on Home.** Home lists what waits on you that you may resolve, each with its reason and one link: a document that cannot publish, with its reason in brief and a link to its Publishing panel showing why; a component holding changes of yours not in a version; a document published from an earlier version; a connection not tested since its credential was set. When nothing waits, it says so. A publication of a baseline is not counted as published from an earlier version.
- **A document opened at its Publishing panel.** `#/documents/{id}/publishing` opens the document with its Publishing panel showing, the panels shown if they were hidden, without changing the panel the document opens at next. `#/documents/{id}/publishing/requests/{request}` shows there a failed publish of yours.
