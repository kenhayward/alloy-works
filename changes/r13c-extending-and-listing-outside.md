### Added

- **Who from outside can reach what.** Administration offers **From outside**: everybody from outside the organisation, what each may read, and every grant reaching them with its expiry. The API lists the same.
- **Extending access from outside.** An administrator extends somebody from outside to a later day, no further than the environment's cap, and the change is audited with both days. A grant to somebody from outside can also be made with its own expiry.
