### Changed

- **Right to left.** In a right-to-left interface language the whole interface lays out right to left:
  its sides, the arrow keys in tabs, toolbars and the outline, a pane's drag, and the icons that point
  along the line. A saved redline carries its content's language and direction.
- **The desktop app in your language.** Its tray and its spelling menu speak your interface language,
  starting in your system's.
