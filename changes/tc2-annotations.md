### Added

- **Newer than pinned.** A component pinned below a newer version you may read says so beside its text, naming both, with **Compare** to see what changed.
- **Notifications on the stream.** Every event stream you hold says `inbox` when a notification arrives, and the published AsyncAPI document describes it beside presence and locks.

### Changed

- **Annotations announced.** Starting and resolving a thread, and sending, accepting, rejecting or withdrawing a suggestion, are said to a screen reader, naming which and where.
- **Annotations without colour.** A thread's anchor has a border beneath it and a redline underlines and strikes through, all kept in a high-contrast mode.
- **Deleted components.** Deleting a component takes everybody in it away as a lost connection does, and its threads in a document that once placed it are listed as orphaned.
