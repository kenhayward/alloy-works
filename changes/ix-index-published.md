### Added

- **The index, published.** Where the layout places an index, which the default layout now does, the PDF, Word and HTML end with one built from the marked entries: sorted in the document's language, sub-entries under their entry, "see" and "see also" in the layout's words, each locator a page in the PDF, an index field in Word and a link to its section in HTML.
- A publish fails, naming the marker, where a "see" or "see also" names a term no marker carries, or where a "see" stands on an entry other markers give pages.
