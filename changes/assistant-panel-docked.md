### Changed

- **The Assistant on one line and docked.** Its name and toolbar share the top line, with **Prompts** there as a popup; the box and what it reads sit on the bottom edge, the box the panel's width and growing upward as you write. **Document** and **Component** are chips, and **Add a part** is always beside them, its finder a popup. **Earlier conversations** is a menu nearly the panel's width, a line each: when, then its title.
