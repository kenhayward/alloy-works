# G3: Generated content in publishing

> T5's third slice, building [generation.md](../design/generation.md)'s "Proposals" as far as
> publishing. **Full tier**: a new publish failure code, a workflow condition, two report routes.
> Built inline, one final review at the slice's end. Decisions marked **(default)** are
> recommendations Ken may overturn.

**Goal:** a publish refused while generated content waits to be accepted, and a gate able to require
none; for any document and any publication, which of its content came from a model, by which run,
model and digest, and who accepted it, kept as long as the content is (GEN-025, GEN-026, GEN-041,
GEN-042, GEN-046). GEN-023 and PUB-082 ask for a prompt's version, which the assistant has none of
until G4, and PUB-082 a record no weaker than a baseline's pins as well: both are served here and
claimed when they are answered whole.

One PR, G3: the refusal, the condition, the two reports, their words and their place on the
Publishing panel and the publication page, the walks, the final review and the close (0.196.0).

## Decisions

| ID   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| G3-A | **What waits is an open generated suggestion** (default; GEN-025, GEN-042): a suggestion with a run and no resolution, on a component the version resolves for the publisher (after its profile, readable), whatever version it was made against. A proposal still open is its requester's draft, not the document's content, and blocks nothing; one rejected, withdrawn or discarded blocks nothing. A publish, and a baseline's making, fails `generated_unaccepted`, resolve stage, a failure a node, its detail how many wait; a preview or a range preview does not, being published nowhere. A baseline already made publishes as pinned: its content is fixed, and a suggestion made since changes none of it. Run read through the row as JSON, so a schema before 0121 reads none.                                                                                                      |
| G3-B | **A gate may require none** (default; GEN-025): a workflow condition `generated`, for documents (on the components the version places) and components (their own), met where no generated suggestion is open: a part of `suggestions`, which requires none of any kind. Additive: a stored definition without it reads as before.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| G3-C | **What came from a model, for a document** (default; GEN-026, GEN-041): `GET /v1/documents/{id}/generated`, on `read`, at its latest version: each placed component the reader may read, with every generated suggestion on it (accepted, open, rejected or withdrawn) and every proposal discarded on it: who asked, who accepted or discarded and when, the run's model, endpoint, instructions version and digest, and for an accepted one the version it went into and whether that version is in the one placed (the same line at or before it, or the main line at or before where the placed line diverged). The placed version says when it was restored from an earlier one. The report names acceptances, not words: a later version holds what was accepted until edited. Open proposals are their requesters' drafts, left out. Unreadable components counted, never named (SCH-032). |
| G3-D | **What came from a model, for a publication** (default): `GET /v1/publications/{id}/generated`, on `read` of the publication, refused to a reader by share alone: the same report over the versions the publication recorded, never against anything newer. Its sources never change or go, so the derivation is a record. No table of its own.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| G3-E | **Kept as long as the content** (GEN-046): runs, proposals, suggestions and resolutions are never deleted (0077, 0120, 0121); erasing the requester and the accepter leaves them, unnamed, and the reports still answer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| G3-F | **Words** (default): the publish failure says how many generated suggestions wait on the part and that accepting or rejecting them in Review ends it; the condition reads "No generated content waits to be accepted".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

## Tasks

Tests: GEN-025 a publish and a baseline's making refused, a preview not, a made baseline still publishing, and a gate requiring none; GEN-042 a rejected one, a discarded proposal and an open one blocking nothing; GEN-026 GEN-041 the document's report, accepted, open and discarded, with run, model and digest; the publication's, a later acceptance not in it; GEN-046 an erased requester and accepter leaving the report answering; the walks; the final review, hunting a name or a run shown to a reader it should not reach.

## Built

G3 with the close (0.196.0) #714, one PR: the page work was small enough to ride with the rest.
Departures, from the pre-flight review: the report reads discarded proposals as well as suggestions,
since a discarded proposal never becomes one; an acceptance on a line counts only up to where the
placed line diverged; GEN-023 and PUB-082 are served but not claimed, a prompt's version arriving with
G4 and PUB-082 asking for pins as well. A reader of a publication who may not read a component it
printed still sees that component's generated items: the publication printed them. A discarded
proposal names who asked to every reader of the document, as GEN-041 asks. The final review found a
GEN-042 test that passed whatever stopped the publish, a report left showing another document's while
the next one loaded, and a doc comment parted from its function; all fixed. Left: the report reads once
and does not follow a suggestion resolved in Review; the line rule is read, not tested, as no suite
here accepts a suggestion on a line.
