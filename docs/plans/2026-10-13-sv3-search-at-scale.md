# SV3: Search at scale

> After [SV](2026-10-13-sv-search-reaches-further.md), on the
> [search-versions spike](../specification/spikes/Search_Versions_Spike_Findings.md), designed in
> [search.md](../design/search.md). **Full tier**: migrations 0090 and 0091, a stored shape
> (`search_history`), the words query rewritten and a widened contract; a pre-flight review and a final
> review. Ken chose on 2026-10-13: build the spike's proposal; filters under every version read current
> values; the backfill may run in the migration (deployments are dev scale; #596 closed); fix the latest
> path's defects in the same slice, as issue #602. Decisions marked **(default)** are recommendations he
> may overturn.

**Goal:** SCH-033's budget - the first page p95 250ms, never above 500ms, at a million components,
whatever the user may see - met for `versions=latest` and `versions=all`, page, total and facets
together (the spike: worst p95 110ms); counts and facets still over what the reader may see, a facet
past the bound a lower bound (SCH-034, SCH-007); nothing leaks on any road (SCH-010, SCH-032).

| PR   | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

| SV3a | This plan; #602's latest-path fixes: words indexable, definitions indexed, ranking and facets bounded with the road chosen, `plan_cache_mode` pinned; migration 0090; the contract's `bounded`. The PR body says `Fixes #602` |
| SV3b | `search_history`, kept by every writer and rebuilt by erasure; `all` reads it, the version named for the page alone; migration 0091 with its backfill; the load suite's search profile; the close (0.165.0)                   |

## Decisions

| ID    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SV3-A | **Words through their index** (#602, cause 1). `websearch_to_tsquery(e.configuration, ...)` becomes one branch per configuration in use: `(e.configuration = 'english'::regconfig and e.vector @@ websearch_to_tsquery('english', $q)) or (...)`, each a constant tsquery a GIN probe serves; the part-of-a-word `or` (UI6) unchanged. The same rows as the per-row form, by construction. **Configurations in use** read in the search's transaction by a skip scan (a recursive `min(configuration) > previous`) over a new btree on `search_entry (configuration)`, and on `search_history`'s in SV3b (default; one probe a configuration, about 1ms). Rejected: a branch for each of `SEARCH_CONFIGURATIONS` (about 30 GIN probes a search, most for nothing). Scoped terms (`title:`, a field) keep their correlated `exists` on `search_text`: per candidate, never the driver. |
| SV3-B | **Definitions by an index** (#602, cause 2): 0090 adds `artifact_version (artifact_id, revision_no desc, version_no desc) where kind in ('field', 'componentType')`, serving `readable_fields` and the component-type facet's names unchanged in their SQL. Indexes in 0090 are created plainly, in its transaction (deployments are dev scale).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| SV3-C | **Ranking bounded** (search.md's unbuilt rule): the candidates are the N most recently changed matches over what the reader may see, ordered `changed_at desc, id desc` (a total order, so both roads and every page pick the same N at a tie), N = 1,000, 1,001 read so "more" is known; then ranked, paged and counted. `SEARCH_RANK_BOUND` beside `SEARCH_COUNT_CAP`. **The trade**: past 1,000 matches the order is the best-ranked of the 1,000 most recently changed, not of all; a better match changed earlier is not shown. Offset paging (at most 1,000 in, as today) stays within the candidates, so SCH-022's order holds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| SV3-D | **The bound comes after every filter the reader set** (default; the spike measured none). Results and the total come from the N newest rows matching the words, the predicate and every filter; so the total is exact to the cap as before ("1,000+" as true as it was), and a filter finding an old match still finds it. Facets come from a second bounded set, the N newest rows failing at most one filter (each facet leaves its own out, SCH-046); with no filter set the two are one scan. Field filters become row predicates on `field_values` (the same `case` as `entry_values`, as an `exists` over the row's own values). Rejected: one candidate set before filters, which drops older filtered matches silently.                                                                                                                                                       |
| SV3-E | **The road chosen by the application**: before the search, `explain (format json)` of the candidates' `where` (predicate, shown, words, scoped terms, filters; no execution) gives the planner's estimate M; walk the `(changed_at, id)` index when M > sqrt(N x rows), rows the source's `pg_class.reltuples` (never analysed, below zero: the indexes road), else find the matches by the word indexes and sort the newest N out of them (ordered by an expression no index holds, `changed_at + interval '0 s'`). Both roads return the same rows; an internal `road` option forces one, for tests only. 0090 adds `search_entry (changed_at, id)`. One estimate per scan; under SV3-D, a filtered search estimates both.                                                                                                                                                          |
| SV3-F | **Facets bounded** (#602, cause 3): every facet aggregates over the facet candidates (SV3-D), so `jsonb_each` unpacks at most 1,001 rows. **The trade**: when the facet candidates reach N, every facet count is a lower bound, shown "at least", and a value held only by older matches is missing altogether; below N every count is exact, as today. SCH-034 is met (a capped count shows itself as a lower bound, applied over what the reader may see); SCH-007 too (over what the reader may see, not over everything it holds).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| SV3-G | **The contract**: the `results` outcome gains `bounded: { ranking: boolean; facets: boolean }`, each whether its candidates passed N. Where `facets` is true, every `FacetValue.capped` is true (its meaning, "the count is a lower bound", unchanged). Descriptions say so; `openapi.json` and the client regenerated. The Search page: facet counts already say "at least" when capped; when `ranking` is bounded, the count line adds "Showing the best of the 1,000 changed most recently." (default copy). Totals and `count`/`capped` unchanged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| SV3-H | **`plan_cache_mode`**: `searchWords` runs `set local plan_cache_mode = force_custom_plan` first; a test reads `current_setting` in its transaction after a search. Every statement is planned for its parameters, the estimate's included.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| SV3-I | **`search_history`** (0091; SV3b): one row per place a component, a document or a section ever had, unique `(artifact_id, node)` nulls not distinct; columns as `search_entry`'s plus `newest_version_id`, no `thread_id`. Vector and body hold every version's words; title, owner, changed, component type, `field_values`, configuration and the flags the newest version's (Ken: filters and facets read current values). A place gone from the newest version keeps its row as its last version left it, found under `all` only. Indexes: GIN vector, trigram body, `(changed_at, id)`, `space_id`, `configuration`. Assets, templates, definitions, publications and threads get no rows: `all` reads them from `search_entry` (SV3-K) (default; so `startThread`, `addComment`, `editComment` and `indexPublication` keep nothing new).                                        |
| SV3-J | **Lexemes once.** A fold `search_history_fold(history tsvector, newer tsvector) = ts_delete(history, tsvector_to_array(newer)) \|\| newer`, an SQL function and an aggregate over it (`order by revision_no, version_no`), both in 0091: a vector grows with distinct words, not versions, positions kept for the newest of each. The body: the newest version's, followed by each word earlier versions held that it does not, once (default; a part of a word in any version is found; a quoted part of several words, `"high dose"`, across versions only in the newest text: the trade). A configuration change keeps earlier words as their versions folded them (rare; stated).                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| SV3-K | **Kept, under the artifact's lock**: `indexVersion`, after `moveEarlier` and `write`, folds each new current row into its place's history row (`insert ... on conflict do update`), incrementally: O(places), never O(versions). `archival.ts` and `deletion.ts` set the flags on history beside `flagEarlier`. Erasure (`reindexNaming`, `rewriteEarlierNaming`) then **rebuilds** each named artifact's history from its current and earlier rows with the aggregate, so a former name leaves it (VER-038); `reindexSearch('whole')` rebuilds every row. One TypeScript function, `rebuildHistory(trx, artifactId?)`, one statement, used by the erasure, the reindex and 0091's run (`REINDEXED_BY`: `'history'`). A test holds fold and rebuild equal after a run of versions.                                                                                                    |
| SV3-L | **`all` reads** `search_history` (`current` false) `union all` `search_entry` rows of the kinds history does not hold (`current` true), disjoint by kind: no collapse, no `distinct on`, no join to `artifact_version` but for the page. A place matches when its words over every version together do (default). **The trades**: two words in two versions find it; `-word` excludes a place any version held it in; a phrase is matched within the fold's positions. The total counts places, so the same as before for one word.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| SV3-M | **The version named, for the page's rows alone**: per page row, a lateral picks the current entry if it matches the whole query, else the newest `search_earlier` row that does, else (the words span versions, SV3-L) the newest version holding any of its words (default). Its version id, number, `latest` and passage (that row's texts) are what the result carries, as SV-G. At most 20 laterals, each by `search_earlier_once`'s prefix.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| SV3-N | **Backfill in 0091's run** (Ken; dev scale): `rebuildHistory` over every place, one `insert ... select` from `search_entry` and `search_earlier` with the aggregate, in the migration's transaction. Pure SQL over the projection, never the chain, so far cheaper than 0088's run; the load suite times it. `latest` and `baseline` never read `search_history`; a baseline stays exact and unbounded (its pins are few).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| SV3-O | **Docs**: search.md's "Ranking is bounded" (N, the order, the roads, the threshold), "Counts and facets" (the facet trade), the plan-cache rule as built, SCH-003's row and "Earlier versions and baselines" (history, the match rule, the version named; #596's gap gone), SCH-033's and SCH-034's rows (how met). The SCH change history gains "From the search-versions spike (SV3), 2026-10-13": no row's text changes; the two trades recorded beside SCH-033 and SCH-034; counts unchanged (default: SCH-034's lower bound covers a facet shown "at least", and a missing value is not a count). No ADR: search.md already holds the bounded rule (default).                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

## Task 1: The latest path - SV3a

- Migration 0090: the definitions' partial index, `search_entry (changed_at, id)`,
  `search_entry (configuration)`. `search-words.ts`: SV3-A to SV3-H, `latest` and `all` alike (`all`
  bounded over today's union until SV3b). The contract, `openapi.json`, the client, the Search page's
  line. A fragment.
- Tests (db, CI):
  - **Index used**: with `enable_seqscan` off in the test's transaction, the words' `EXPLAIN` holds a
    Bitmap Index Scan on `search_entry_vector`, and the facets' on the definitions' index; the per-row
    form cannot, so this fails before the fix. No ID: it demonstrates the mechanism, not the budget.
  - Two configurations (`english`, `simple`) each found by a word, as before.
  - **Two roads, one answer**: 1,100 matches written to the projection directly (as SCH-034's test
    does), same `changed_at` for a run of them; `road: 'walk'` and `road: 'indexes'` return the same
    page, total and facets, every page.
  - **`SCH-010`** a reader of nothing matching gets no result, a zero total and empty facets, one literal
    title per road and scope (walk, indexes; latest, all), filtered and not.
  - **`SCH-034`** (the existing test rewritten): Ivy's 900 exact; Ada's 2,100 a capped total, each facet
    `capped`, `bounded.facets` true, the bound over what she may see.
  - A filter finding a match older than the newest 1,000 (SV3-D); past N, a better-ranked old match is
    not on the first page (SV3-C's trade, stated in the title); `plan_cache_mode` pinned.
- Full run's browser walks (UI change: one line).

## Task 2: Every version from one row a place - SV3b, and the close

- Migration 0091: `search_history`, its indexes and grants, the fold and aggregate, `REINDEXED_BY`.
  `search-history.ts` (fold, `rebuildHistory`); `indexVersion`, archival, deletion, erasure, reindex;
  `all` over history (SV3-L, SV3-M).
- Tests (db, CI): every SV1 `SCH-003`/`SCH-058` test passes unchanged; fold equals rebuild after
  versions, a lexeme held once whatever its versions; a place gone from the newest version found under

  deleted out of `all`; **`VER-038`** an erased name found under no scope; the version named is the
  newest that matches, and the spanning case names the newest holding a word; **`SCH-010`** over
  history, both roads; the `all` SQL holds no `distinct on` and reads no `search_earlier` but in the
  page's lateral (its text pinned); a tenant migrated through 0091 holds what `rebuildHistory` makes.
- **The load suite** (default): `packages/db/src/load/search.load.ts`, outside `pnpm test` as the
  version chain's is. It migrates a tenant and writes the spike's profile into the real tables by SQL

  total and facets, 20 warm runs. At every scale it checks results and counts against ground truth and
  the road taken; **`SCH-033`**'s title asserts p95 at most 250ms and every run under 500ms only at a
  million, run by hand at the close (about 25GB, Docker), its numbers recorded below and in search.md.
  It also reports the backfill's time, history's bytes against `search_entry`'s, and a write's cost.
  Rejected: the budget in CI (a million rows take 15 to 25 minutes to make).
- The close: `features.md`, `README.md`, `architecture.md`, the CHANGELOG, 0.165.0, the baseline.

## What the final review hunts

- A result, total or facet reaching a row the reader may not read on either road, or the bound applied
  before the predicate (its stopping point a signal; SCH-032).
- The two roads disagreeing: ties, nulls, a filter only one applies, page two taking the other road.
- A filter or facet losing a match it should hold under SV3-D; `bounded` false while a facet is short.
- History diverging from its rows: a writer of `search_entry` or `search_earlier` (moves, the flags,
  erasure, reindex, restore) not keeping it; an erased name left in a folded vector; a race between two
  versions of one artifact outside its lock.
- The `latest` statement reading history or a union; the estimate run without `plan_cache_mode` set.
- A history vector nearing a tsvector's 1MB or positions saturating at 16,383 on a deep document.

## Recommendations for Ken

- **SV3-D** (bound after the reader's filters, two scans when filtered) over the spike's one scan.
- **SV3-I/K** (`all` reads threads, assets, templates, definitions and publications from `search_entry`,
  so history is kept only where versions are) over a history row for everything.
- **SV3-J** (body as distinct earlier words) and **SV3-L/M** (the union matches; the version named
  falls back to one holding a word) as the trades stated.
- **The load suite** carrying SCH-033's test, run at a million by hand at the close.
