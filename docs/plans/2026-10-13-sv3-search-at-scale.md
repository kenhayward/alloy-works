> After [SV](2026-10-13-sv-search-reaches-further.md), on the
> [search-versions spike](../specification/spikes/Search_Versions_Spike_Findings.md), designed in
> [search.md](../design/search.md). **Full tier**: migrations 0090 and 0091, a stored shape
> (`search_history`), the words query rewritten and a widened contract; a pre-flight review and a final
> review. Ken chose on 2026-10-13: build the spike's proposal; filters under every version read current
> values; the backfill may run in the migration (deployments are dev scale; #596 closed); fix the latest
> path's defects in the same slice, as issue #602. Decisions marked **(default)** are recommendations he
> may overturn.

**Goal:** SCH-033's budget - the first page p95 250ms, never above 500ms, at a million components,
whatever the user may see - met for `versions=latest` and `versions=all`, page, total and facets
together (the spike: worst p95 110ms); counts and facets still over what the reader may see, a facet
past the bound a lower bound (SCH-034, SCH-007); nothing leaks on any road (SCH-010, SCH-032).

## What the final review hunts

- A result, total or facet reaching a row the reader may not read on either road, or the bound applied
  before the predicate (its stopping point a signal; SCH-032).
- The two roads disagreeing: ties, nulls, a filter only one applies, page two taking the other road.
- A filter or facet losing a match it should hold under SV3-D; `facetsBounded` false while a facet is short.
- History diverging from its rows: a writer of `search_entry` or `search_earlier` (moves, the flags,
  erasure, reindex, restore) not keeping it; an erased name left in a folded vector; a race between two
  versions of one artifact outside its lock.
- The `latest` statement reading history or a union; the estimate run without `plan_cache_mode` set.
- A history vector nearing a tsvector's 1MB or positions saturating at 16,383 on a deep document.

## Recommendations for Ken

- **SV3-D** (bound after the reader's filters, two scans when filtered) over the spike's one scan.
- **SV3-I/K** (`all` reads threads, assets, templates, definitions and publications from `search_entry`,
  so history is kept only where versions are) over a history row for everything.
- **SV3-J** (body as distinct earlier words) and **SV3-L/M** (the union matches; the version named
  falls back to one holding a word) as the trades stated.
- **The load suite** carrying SCH-033's test, run at a million by hand at the close.
