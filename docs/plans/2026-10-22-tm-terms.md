# TM: Terms

> T6's third slice ([terms.md](../design/terms.md)). **Full tier**: an artifact kind and a workflow
> kind, a theme catalogue version, a published-document schema and template, the editor's first
> suggestion as you type. Ken's answers of 2026-10-22 are marked **(Ken)**; the rest **(default)**. The
> pre-flight review's findings are folded in.

**Goal:** an author applies governed terms, by a Term action or by typing an abbreviation; a document
prints each term's forms by its theme's rule, first use expanded, with a glossary and a list of
abbreviations; search reaches content through a term's labels and narrower terms; deprecated terms are
flagged.

## Decisions

| ID   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| TM-A | **A term is a space's artifact on workflow** (Ken; LIB-049): `term`, its standing a workflow kind of its own (draft, approved, deprecated) following `prompt`'s (0123); deprecated read from its standing, not a designation. `replacedBy` names a term of the same space, checked at save for a cycle and a chain of at most five.                             |
| TM-B | **Vocabularies to T7** (Ken): LIB-024, LIB-058 and MET-032 move with a change history, beside the fields that draw on them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| TM-C | **The term's forms print** (Ken; LIB-016): the latest approved revision's forms, by the theme's rule; a draft, unreadable or missing term prints the covered words and is reported; a deprecated one prints, flagged in the report. The printed run takes the other marks of the span's first character.                                                        |
| TM-D | **Expansion is the theme's** (Ken; LIB-017): a term catalogue version, `expand: firstUse \| always \| never`, the default theme `firstUse`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| TM-E | **First use** (default): resolved in reading order after transclusion, conditions and profiling, at the stage citations are placed, footnotes at their reference; a heading, the contents, bookmarks and running heads always print the preferred label and never count as a use; a component reused in two documents prints by each. A split span is two uses. |
| TM-F | **Labels by language** (default; LIB-050): the document language's, else the base language's, each fallback listed in the publication's report by term and language.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| TM-G | **Pinned** (default): the term versions a publish reads are its inputs and a baseline's pins, as CI's entries are.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| TM-H | **Glossary and abbreviations** (default; PUB-039): `matter.glossary` and `matter.abbreviations` in the layout, a new default layout version placing both, generated from the approved terms the document uses, headed and listed for tagging (PDF `L`/`LI`, Word styled paragraphs).                                                                            |
| TM-I | **Search** (default; LIB-026, SCH-015, SCH-056): terms indexed as artifacts; a query's words that are a term's label or abbreviation reach the content using that term and its narrower terms (three levels at most) through the reference index, at query time, so nothing goes stale.                                                                         |
| TM-J | **SKOS** (default; LIB-028, LIB-029, IMP-015): exported as JSON-LD in a documented compact form; imported from that form and from Turtle (by `n3`, its MIT licence checked); what is not represented - predicates, unknown language tags, relations to terms outside the file - reported.                                                                       |
| TM-K | **The mark** (default): `term` is a term's identity; a save refuses a new or changed mark naming no term the author may read in the component's space, as CI-H does; a footnote or cross-reference inside a term span is refused by the editor.                                                                                                                 |
| TM-L | **Suggested as you type** (Ken; LIB-019): when the word before the caret matches an abbreviation or label of an approved term in the space, a combobox offers it; Tab or Enter applies the mark as one undo step, Esc dismisses; nothing during IME composition; announced; labels held in a per-space index fetched once, capped.                              |

## Risks

- **Typing suggestions** are the editor's first: their focus and announcement get the walks.
- **A renamed term** changes every document's next publish, never a baseline's.

## Built

TM in three PRs: TMa #767, TMb #768 and the close #769. Departures: a term is deprecated by its
standing's state key, no new flag; the abbreviation is SKOS's `notation`; the expansion rule is a
`term` catalogue (catalogue/5); a label falls back through a language serving the document's (`en`
for `en-GB`) before the base; the Term action is Mod-Alt-g; `definedTerm` no longer extends as one
types past it. Two automated security reviews and the final review found no leak; they fixed an
import merging into terms its importer could not read or edit, a lock order two term writers could
deadlock on, an import clearing a replacement it did not resolve, a Word run with two property sets,
SKOS limits enforced only after the whole parse, an accepted proposal making a component unchecked,
search walking narrower terms its reader could not read, an unreadable replacement's id answered, an
archive guard counting removed components, a replacement chain lengthened from behind, Enter taken by
an offer the author never chose, the term index fetched once per editor rather than per document, and
one unresolved use hiding a term at every node. Left, named: section titles are not in the reference
index, so a term used only there does not hold back its archive; a use a condition removes is still
counted in warnings; the Review panel does not list deprecated terms; a footnote's editor offers no
suggestions; a footnote or cross-reference inside a term span is not refused by the editor.
