# WF5: Effective and review-by dates

> T9's fifth phase ([ADR-0061](../decisions/0061-t9s-order.md)), extending
> [workflow.md](../design/workflow.md). **Full tier**: a migration (0099), stored shapes, new routes and
> a worker sweep. Built inline, one final review at the phase's end (Ken, 2026-10-14). Decisions marked
> **(default)** are recommendations Ken may overturn.

**Goal:** a component or document carries an effective date and a review-by date with a named owner;
the owner is prompted before review falls due; content past review says so wherever it is used; the
workflow declares what a lapsed review does; and a revision approved to take effect later is served
only once it does, its taking effect an audited act nobody performs (LIF-015 to LIF-018, LIF-059,
LIF-060).

## Decisions

| ID    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WF5-A | **Dates are acts** (LIF-015, LIF-016): insert-only `artifact_dates (artifact_id, kind, effective_at, effective_offset, review_by, owner, by, at)`, the latest row the artifact's dates, set by `PUT /v1/{components\|documents}/{id}/dates` with edit, audited `dates.set`. `effective_at` an instant with the setter's offset kept (LIF-062); `review_by` a calendar date; `owner` a principal who may read it. Components and documents only (default). |
| WF5-B | **An effective date binds at approval** (LIF-060): a gate designating a revision while the artifact's effective date is in the future records `revision_effective (version_id, effective_at)`, insert-only, so the revision and its date are fixed; any change is a new version, a draft through the gate again (WF-F). Past or absent: effective as it is approved.                                                                                      |
| WF5-C | **Not served before it takes effect** (default): `latestRevisionsOf`, which resolves every `approved` reference, takes the latest revision whose effective date has passed. The artifact's own state is Approved as the gate passes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| WF5-D | **Taking effect is the sweep's** (LIF-060): each sweep records `revision_effected (version_id, at)` and `revision.effective { kind, revision, version, effectiveAt }` for every revision whose date has passed, as the system. Served from the instant itself, whenever the sweep runs.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| WF5-E | **The review policy** (LIF-059): a workflow body's optional `review: { remind: 0 to 365 days, lapse: visibility \| move to a state \| escalate to a person }`, absent read as 30 days and visibility (LIF-Q06, ADR-0061).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| WF5-F | **Prompted** (LIF-017): Home's attention shows **Review due** to the owner from `remind` days before the date (default: no notification kind, which would need an actor the system has not).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| WF5-G | **A lapse acts once** (LIF-059): the sweep records `review_lapse (artifact_id, review_by, action)` and `review.lapsed` the first time a date passes unmet; `move` moves it by no one (`transition: null`); `escalate` shows **Review overdue** on the named person's Home. A new review-by date starts again.                                                                                                                                             |
| WF5-H | **Past review is shown wherever it is used** (LIF-018): a `review` on the component and document views, `pastReview` on list items and on each occurrence in a document's texts, each a lozenge, **Past review**, in the header, the lists and the label of every reference to it.                                                                                                                                                                        |

## Task 1: Dates, effect and lapse - WF5a

- 0099: the four tables, grants, the audit kinds. db: `dates.ts` (set, read in bulk), the designation's
  effect, `latestRevisionsOf` by effect, the sweep's two halves, attention's two kinds. Domain: the
  review policy. Contract, routes, the worker's sweep.
- Tests: dates set, refused and audited; a future-effective revision fixed at approval, unserved until
  its date, served and audited once after; the policy's rules; each lapse once; attention for owner and
  escalation; the views' and texts' flags; the migration on an old schema.

  Administration > Workflows; attention's wording. The final review hunts a revision served early, a
  lapse acting twice, and a date or owner shown to someone who may not read it. The close: 0.170.0.
