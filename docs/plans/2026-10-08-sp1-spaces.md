# SP1: Spaces made, renamed and archived

> The first T3 slice ([ADR-0045](../decisions/0045-t3-is-the-collaboration.md), ADM-049), ahead of the
> audit log. [access.md](../design/access.md#spaces) already designs creating and renaming a space;
> this plan adds archiving and builds all three. **Full tier, one PR**: a migration and an act on
> tenancy, so a pre-flight review (done, folded in) and a final review. Ken may overrule.

**Goal:** an administrator of the environment makes a space, renames one and archives or restores one,
from Administration's **Spaces** and through the API; nothing new can be made in an archived space,
and everything already in it stays as it was. Who may do each is the access model's.

## Task 1: The model and the routes (`packages/db`, `apps/service`, `packages/api-contract`)

- 0059; `spaces.ts` (`createSpace` checked by SP-B, `renameSpace`, `archiveSpace` with SP-D,
  `restoreSpace`); `createArtifact`'s check and lock (SP-C) and each route mapping `SpaceArchived`;
  the listing (SP-E); the routes, wire codes and words (SP-F); `dev-content.ts` (SP-I); the access
  harnesses' entries for the new routes (`OTHER_TENANT_IDS` and `VALID_INPUT` in
  `cross-tenant.test.ts`, `HOLDING_NOTHING` in `access-routes.test.ts`); `openapi.json` and the client
  regenerated.
- Tests: **`ADM-049`** an administrator of the environment makes, renames, archives and restores a
  space through the API, and one who administers only a space is refused each. Uncited: a name taken,
  empty, overlong, with a control character, and NFD normalised; the last space, and two archives at
  once; a component, a document (blank and from a template), a template, a connection and a query
  definition made in an archived space refused `space_archived`, and editing, adding an image to,
  publishing and refreshing a binding in what is already there still allowed; a creation racing an
  archive; the listing's `archived`, `mayCreate` and `?archived=false`; a token without `administer`
  refused; the seed after General is renamed.

## Task 2: The page (`apps/web`)

- Administration's Spaces (SP-G); Connections and New query definition changed to leave archived
  spaces out (SP-E); New component and New document checked to; words dash-free.
- Tests (jsdom): **`ADM-049`** an administrator makes, renames, archives and restores a space from
  Spaces. Archiving the last space shows the refusal; each pick list leaves an archived space out.

## Task 3: The whole system, docs

- `tests/browser`: by keyboard, make a space, rename it, archive it, see it gone from New component's
  choice, restore it; axe on the dialog. `tests/e2e`: the same over HTTP, and a component made in an
  archived space refused.
- Docs: access.md (archiving, SP-C and SP-D, and ADM-049 in its Requirements owned), features.md and
  the README, a change fragment, this plan's row. Not a close: T3's slices close together.
