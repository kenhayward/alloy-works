# W11: Recovery

> **A sketch**, built a pull request at a time, each test-first with one final whole-branch review
> before it opens that is asked for a break of its own against every citation. It builds W11 of
> [the rest of T1](2026-09-25-t1-remainder.md) from component-editor.md's
> [Recovery, as W11 builds it](../design/component-editor.md#recovery-as-w11-builds-it) and
> storage-and-versioning.md's VER-003 and VER-004, whose decisions RC-A to RC-H were taken as
> recommended on Ken's instruction of 2026-09-28 to continue, and are his to review.

**Goal:** nothing an author saw saved is lost to a closed tab, a reload or a crash; they can see and
restore what they saved; undo survives a reload; and iterations are kept exactly as long as the tenant
says after the next cut, then swept.

## Global constraints

- Test titles cite only what they show, checked with `pnpm trace show <ID>`, in a literal title.
- Each test is watched fail: a new test before the code, or, where the code exists, by breaking it.
- No em or en dash in user-facing text, and no real names, addresses or paths in a fixture.
- `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm format`, the affected suites, then `pnpm trace generate` after
  Prettier and `pnpm trace pins`; the full suite before each pull request, and its CI log read.
- Iterations stay immutable (VER-001): nothing in W11 updates a row, and the only delete is the sweep's.
- A migration is checked against every write path the stored shape admits.

## W11.1: Retention

1. Migration: a singleton `editing_policy` with `iteration_retention_days` (30; 1 to 365); `expires_at`
   dropped from `iteration`; an index for the sweep; the runtime role granted `delete` on `iteration`
   behind a trigger that refuses any row whose opened-from version has no successor cut more than the
   window ago.
2. `GET` and `PUT /v1/settings/editing`, `administer` on the tenant.
3. The worker's interval sweep removes iterations past their window in every tenant.
4. Tests: VER-003 (kept while no later version exists however old; kept for the window after the next
   cut; swept after it; the trigger refuses an early delete), VER-004 (the default stated and read; set by
   an administrator, refused to anyone else; the sweep follows a changed window).

**W11.1, as built.** Migration 0037 makes `editing_policy` one row, as `access_policy` is, its
`iteration_retention_days` 30 by default and checked from 1 to 365; the runtime role reads it and may
update that column alone. `iteration.expires_at` and its check are gone, and nothing read them but the
insert and the VER-001 test, which now says the runtime role cannot change an iteration nor remove one
before its window. `ITERATION_RETENTION_DAYS` stays as the default's constant, which a test holds to the
column's default. The runtime role may delete an iteration behind `iteration_swept_only`, which finds the
next cut as the first version after the opened-from one by `(revision_no, version_no)` and refuses the
delete unless that version's `created_at` and the window are at or before `now()`; `artifact_version`
has no trigger, so the tests move a cut back as the administrator. `sweepIterations` deletes by the same
condition, and `iterationRetained` states it as a SQL expression over an alias, for W11.2's listing. The
two routes are `GET /v1/settings/editing`, to any session, since a window only an administrator could
read would not be stated, and `PUT`, `administer` at the tenant, whose out-of-range body the contract's
schema answers `400 invalid_request` before the handler; `setEditingPolicy` refuses the same range as
`retention.out_of_range`, and the handler answers that as the same `400`. The access and cross-tenant
harnesses each needed the `PUT`'s entry. The worker's `sweepExpiredIterations` runs on the sign-in
sweep's interval; its test breaks one tenant by revoking its delete grant.

## W11.2: Recovery

1. `GET /v1/components/{id}/iterations` (a cursor, newest first, no content) and
   `GET /v1/components/{id}/iterations/{iteration}`, both for the caller's own rows while they hold the
   lock, and only rows the sweep would keep.
2. The component's `GET` answers its caller the time of their newest iteration opened from the latest
   version (RC-F).
3. The editor: **Recover** offered above the text; a Recovery state and panel listing the iterations,
   restoring one after flushing the current state as an iteration; history and stored steps cleared.
4. Tests: CNT-174 (the writer reads under the lock; another holder, a reader and the writer without the
   lock are refused), CNT-090 (listed newest first and restored, content and values), CNT-067 (after a
   closed tab, what was saved is offered and restored).

**W11.2, as built.** `packages/db/src/recovery.ts` holds the three reads. `listIterations` pages a
principal's own iterations of one component through the listings' `keysetPage`, over a new `iterations`
listing sorted by `created_at` descending and then the id, joined to the version each was opened from
for its number, and filtered by `iterationRetained`, so it answers exactly what the sweep keeps; it
takes a snapshot for the cursor's shape but filters nothing by it, as `listPeople` does not, since an
iteration is never changed, only swept. `readIteration` answers one whole under the same filter, and
nothing alike for another principal's, another component's, a swept one or an id that is not one.
`newestUncutIteration` answers the `created_at` of the principal's newest iteration opened from the
latest version, and **null where that iteration's digest is the latest version's content and values**,
a refinement of RC-F: text changed and changed back, then released with nothing to cut, would otherwise
be offered as unsaved work every time the component opened. The two routes are
`GET /v1/components/{id}/iterations` and `GET /v1/components/{id}/iterations/{iteration}`, `edit` on the
component, and **each names the asking session in a `session` query**, as `DELETE .../lock` does, since
`holding` needs the session and the design's table named no way to carry it; a session that does not hold
the lock is refused through the same mapping as a save, `lock_held` or `lock_required`, and any iteration
id not the caller's own is a 404. The listing uses `pageAsked` and `cursorFor`, and API-007's walk over
every listing includes it, over two iterations the test saves. `GET /v1/components/{id}` gains `unsaved`,
`{ savedAt }` or null, and creating answers null. The access and cross-tenant harnesses each needed both
routes. In the renderer, the session has a seventh phase, `recovery`, which the surface does not take
changes in: `recover()` claims **always afresh and always with `move`**, from reading or from a
`recoverable` lost - afresh because the id a reload keeps may already have saves past this page's
count, which the first save after a restore would then find stale; with `move` because the author asked
to recover here, and a lock another author holds refuses it all the same, naming them. A claim into
Recovery with unsent changes schedules their save at once, so what was on screen is usually saved
before the author chooses; `restore` forces the flush again and waits for it, then reads, then calls the
component's `open`, which runs `readContent` - the migration chain and the parse, CNT-012 and CNT-013 -
and `toEditor`, refuses by the iteration's time what will not read or will not edit, and otherwise puts
the text in a fresh state, so the history goes with it, and the values in the fields; the restored text is
then sent at once. The adapter answers each listed row as this window's where its session is the one the
adapter holds now. `RecoveryPanel` is a region in the `F6` ring, focused as it opens, closed by `Escape`
or **Close**, with **Restore** named by the time to the second and **Show older** moving the focus to the
first row it adds; the focus goes back to the text as it closes. The notice above the text is a new
`Notice` tone, `unsaved`, with the `--warn` edge. Not built: Recovery in a window that lost the lock to
a move or to another author, and an offer of saves opened from a version older than the latest - both
named in [Two windows, one author](../design/component-editor.md#two-windows-one-author).

## W11.3: Undo across a reload

1. Session storage per component: the opened-from version and document, the steps with their history
   grouping, the values, the last sequence sent.
2. The component's `GET` answers the caller's own session its latest accepted sequence.
3. On reload: claimed again under the same session where nobody else holds the lock, the steps replayed,
   anything past the service's sequence sent; another holder means Reading with the changes offered as
   text; a version cut since discards the steps.
4. The title editor's history without a depth limit.
5. Tests: CNT-069 (undo after a reload reaches back to where the session opened), CNT-067 (a reload
   loses nothing past the last acknowledged save), CNT-169 (steps from before a cut discarded).
