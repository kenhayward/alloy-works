# RV: Revised values

> T3's sixth phase, its second half ([ADR-0057](../decisions/0057-t3s-order-and-template-moves-leave-for-t9.md)),
> building [bindings.md](../design/bindings.md#a-value-revised-by-hand)'s "A value revised by hand"
> (BI-O, B5) under [ADR-0036](../decisions/0036-revising-a-bound-value-by-hand-moves-to-t3.md), and
> the bound cell's half [tables.md](../design/tables.md) left to T3. **Full tier**: a migration,
> stored shapes, new contracts, the publish's record; a pre-flight review (its findings folded in:
> RV-A, RV-D and RV-E were reshaped by it) and a final review. Ken asked for the run on 2026-10-12; decisions marked **(default)** are
> recommendations he may overturn.

**Goal:** an author corrects a bound value, inline or a bound table's cell, in place and with a
reason, without touching the query or the binding; the query's value is kept behind it, the revision
is marked as a person's wherever the product shows it, a source revision meeting it is decided
deliberately, and provenance gives both halves.

## What is there

D3, B1 to B4, B6 and TB1 to TB3 built the rest: `binding_resolution` (0047, 0049) with `lockBindings`
and the service-side `replaces` precondition; `publication_request_binding` and `publication_binding`
held whole by `publication_recorded_whole` (0050); `bind` replacing an inline binding with plain text
and laying a bound table out by `layoutTable`; `provenance.json` at schema 3; the Data tab, Value panel
and provenance panel; one marker, _revision waiting_. The audit kind `binding.revised` is declared,
with no detail and no emitter. Nothing stores or reads a revision.

## Final review

Fixed in RV3: a kept revision named the person who kept it, not who entered it (H1); one cell named by
two spellings of its key could hold two revisions (H2); Keep carried an inline revision across a
changed take (M1, now only across a changed mode); a kept value not of its new column failed a whole
resolve and wedged a pending result (M2, now that binding's failure); a request could read a
resolution and the revisions in front of it across another act's commit (M4, now under
`lockBindings`); the rows route and the stage disagreed on which cells a revision applies to (M5, one
`cellRevisable`, and the Data tab says a column no longer revisable); an own view with a revision
could not be resolved from the page (M6); a carried withdrawal reused the revision's reason (L6);
lengths counted before NFC (L8); the Review count counted bindings (L2). Left: the decision and the
dialog format by the product's formats, not the theme's (L3); the Provenance panel shows a revised
cell's revision without the query's value for it, which `provenance.json` gives (L5); confirm and the
pending finish deciding are not tested beside accept and resolve.

## Risks

- **A lapsed revision** (RV-B) is the one way a revision stops standing without a decision: an edit
  to the binding changes its question. It is listed, never silent, but nothing refuses the edit.
- **A revised cell leaves the query's subtotals as they were**: the panel says so; totals are the
  query's (ADR-0045).
- **Not claimed**: DAT-061 (its gate, T9). DAT-057 is claimed but for an image: a bound image is not
  revised by hand, a gap named beside the claims.
- **A value the source does not give** - a null, an empty text - cannot be revised: a revision stands
  in front of a value. Filling one by hand is a later decision, named in bindings.md.
