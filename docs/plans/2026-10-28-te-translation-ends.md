> **The requirements are partly thin, partly too big:**
>
> - **LOC-039 is not an open end.** A message form is a new inline node whose arms are the plural
>   categories of each variant's own language (English two, Arabic six), through the editor, XLIFF
>   (2.1 has no plural module), the PDF, Word and HTML: an IX-sized slice. Its own slice, **MF**, next in T6
>   (Ken). Bindings exist (B1 to B6), so nothing blocks it.
> - **LOC-036's "versioned like any other configuration"** describes nothing built: every tenant setting
>   is a singleton updated in place, audited by name and never value (AU1-I, `settings.ts`), and
>   ADM-001 (T7) says nothing of versions. The rest is met: all four policies are declared tenant
>   settings (0160, 0164, 0165) whose defaults the contract states (`settings.ts`'s
>   `TranslationSettings`). Only "versioned" is open: Ken made it the policy's own
>   history.
> - **LOC-016's "wherever a component is used"** is met where it is placed (a document's reference
>   rows, TR-M) and on its header; TR named search results and the listing, which show a component
>   rather than use it, and the space report's 500 cap is not a report. Both built here, cheaply.
> - **LOC-038 is bigger than UI left it.** No list of content languages exists: editor-02's language
>   picker, STY-048's scripts and bindings' locale data (bindings.md) wait on it, and the worker's ICU,
>   which sorts every glossary, index and bibliography, is unrecorded. A tab's collation cannot be read
>   from a page, so Ken's answer is LOC-044: a tab's own sorts are the browser's, said so.
> - **LOC-029** is the interface's: a publication's layout flows, and Typst's overflow is
>   publishing's to measure, not this walk's.

**Goal:** a component's translation status shows wherever it is listed and in a space's paged report
(LOC-016); the translation policy keeps its history and a publish names the one it ran under (LOC-036);
the product declares its content languages and records every runtime's locale data (LOC-044); the
pseudo walk opens every dialog and measures overlap (LOC-029).

## Risks

- **Listing latency**: `translationsOf` adds one query a page, its correlated count per line; measured
  against the listing before TEa merges.
- **Overlap's false positives**: focus rings, badges on icons and a dialog over its page; measured on
  the walk first, each exemption named.
- **A dialog walk grows long**: about forty dialogs, two locales; the walk stays one run that names
  every failure (`expect.soft`), and is timed in TEd.
- **The report's contract changes** (`capped` gone): pre-release, the drift check names it.

## Built

TE in four PRs: TEa #808, TEb #811, TEc #812 and the close #814. Departures: CLDR is 48.2.0, not
47.0.0, FormatJS's own pin (TE-E, amended); the service's locale data is on `GET /v1/about`, signed
in, not the public `GET /v1/tenant` (TE-F, amended). TEb fixed Administration reading a section
opened by its address as read-only (#810). The census found 49 dialog files: 41 walked, 8 named
(each needs a data source, an extension, a worker's publication or a lapsed lock). Overlap found two,
both fixed in CSS, none marked `data-overlaps`; the walk also found every Modal throwing in a
pseudo-locale (its selectors were catalogue strings) and the components cursor racing a language
change. The walk went from about 6 s to 47 s. Left open: about 18 other `everyPage` callers share
that race, and in pseudo the side panel covers the Overrides button, which the walk presses by key.
