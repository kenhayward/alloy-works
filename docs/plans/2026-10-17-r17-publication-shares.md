# R17: Publication shares

> T4's seventeenth slice, extending [publishing.md](../design/publishing.md),
> [access.md](../design/access.md) and [audit.md](../design/audit.md). **Full tier**: access reaching
> outside the tenant, a migration (0114) and new routes. Built inline, one final review at the slice's
> end. Decisions marked **(default)** are recommendations Ken may overturn; the rest are his answers of
> 2026-10-17.

**Goal:** a publisher shares one publication with a named person outside the tenant, who signs in to
read it until the share expires or is revoked; every access is recorded and reportable, and a
superseded publication says so.

## Decisions

| ID    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| R17-A | **An invited outside person** (Ken; PUB-057). A share names an address: an external principal who signed in with it, verified, is taken; otherwise the address is invited as external, the invitation lasting as long as the share. An address that is one of the tenant's own members is refused (`share.member`), and so is one an administrator's invitation waits for holding anything but shares (`share.invitation_waiting`), so a share never revives what an administrator let lapse (the pre-flight review). Identity is proved by signing in through OIDC with a verified address, as every invitation is: the organisation's provider, which may hold guests, or Google where the tenant permits it; nothing is anonymous and no link reads without a sign-in. Sign-in returns to the publication it started from (R17b). |
| R17-B | **A share is an ordinary grant** (default; PUB-084, IAM-049, IAM-071): an allow at the publication artifact, so the readable set, expiry, revocation and the external-access listing (IAM-051) hold it unchanged. Its role is **Shared reader**, `read` alone, marked `fixed` by 0114 and refused change or removal by a trigger, so neither a tenant nor a migration widening roles by name widens a share; the grants route refuses it (`grant.role_fixed`), so a share is only ever made as one. A share reaches that publication alone: a grant at an artifact reaches nothing else (decision D).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| R17-C | **Who may share**: `publish` on the publication's document and `read` on the publication (default), decided under the epoch held for a change. Sharing is a publisher's release of what they published; an external principal never holds `publish` (IAM-047). A publisher's share invites the address without `administer`, but what it invites can reach nothing beyond the share (IAM-071). Revoking removes a shared-reader grant at that publication and nothing else.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| R17-D | **30 days, capped at 90** (Ken; PUB-058): the tenant's external default and cap, already 30 and 90 (IAM-049), so a share takes them and an administrator's policy moves both. Revoking removes the grant; a decision reads it at every request.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| R17-E | **Revoked at once means the bytes too** (default; PUB-058): somebody from outside is given no signed store link at all - outputs, veraPDF's report and `provenance.json` alike are served by `GET /v1/publications/{id}/outputs/{format}`, deciding `read` at each request, saved as an attachment - rather than a link that would outlive a revocation by its five minutes. Members keep the signed links.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| R17-I | **An outsider sees the share alone** (the pre-flight review; PUB-084): every route a session alone opens is walked as a share-only principal, and what lists the tenant's people, spaces or anything unshared answers them nothing; a publication's bindings, which name datasets and authors, are refused them. R17b hides the document and baseline links from them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| R17-F | **Every access recorded** (PUB-060, LIF-070): a recipient reading the publication or one of its outputs is audited `publication.shared_accessed` (`{publication, output}`), so who, when and which are the event's. **Reported on the publication and in Administration** (Ken): `GET /v1/publications/{id}/shares` lists each share and its accesses for whoever may share; the audit log and its CSV hold them all.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| R17-G | **Told, keeps the old** (Ken; PUB-059): a publication answers `superseded` - its document published later, under the same profile, from a later version (so neither a variant nor an old baseline republished supersedes it; the pre-flight review) - and the newer one's id only where the reader may read it. A share never follows to the newer one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| R17-H | **Left**: PUB-081 (a share stops resolving on tenant closure) waits for closure; a share is not sent by email - the publisher sends the publication's address; sharing again with somebody shared with already is refused (`share.duplicate`), extended from Administration as any outside grant is (IAM-050).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Tasks

- **R17a**: 0114 (the fixed role, `role.fixed`, the audit kind's detail); `sharePublication`, `listShares`, `revokeShare` and their routes, each joining the route registries; the streamed output route, and no signed link to anybody from outside; `shared_accessed` emitted from the read and the output; `superseded` on the view; the outsider's walk of every session route. Tests: a share invites and grants that publication alone, refuses a member, a waiting invitation and past the cap; a revoked share reads nothing at once; each access audited and listed; a fixed role refused change and the grants route; superseded by profile and version, the newer id by readability; the walk.
- **R17b**: the page's Shares panel and dialog, the accesses, the recipient's notice and output links, the document and baseline hidden from them, Outside naming the publication; sign-in by Google where permitted, returning to the publication. Browser walks. The final review; the close.

## Built

R17a #696, and R17b with the close (0.188.0) #697. Departures: the pre-flight review added R17-I (an
outsider sees the share alone, held by a walk of every route a session opens), the refusal of a share
reviving an administrator's invitation, the grants route refusing the fixed role, and supersession by
profile; it also asked that a share be refused where no route admits outsiders, declined, since an
organisation's provider may hold guests. R17b added `sharesOnly` and `outside` to `/v1/me`, the
environment's ways in to `/v1/tenant`, and names what an artifact grant is on in the external-access
listing. The final review found `provenance.json` naming people to a share-only reader, an invitation
shortened by a later share and left behind by an extension, a share that had ended blocking another,
the dialog's dates past the cap across a change of clocks, and the page showing a member's links before
it knew the reader; all fixed. Left: a guest who signed in uninvited through the organisation's provider
is a member, so is refused a share; the dialog holds the default and cap at 30 and 90 rather than the
environment's own; a share's accesses are listed whole; a publisher can learn whether an address is a
member's; PUB-081 waits for tenant closure.
