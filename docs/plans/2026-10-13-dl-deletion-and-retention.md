# DL: Deletion and retention

> T3's eighth phase, its third slice ([ADR-0057](../decisions/0057-t3s-order-and-template-moves-leave-for-t9.md),
> [ADR-0060](../decisions/0060-deletion-is-a-tombstone-and-retention-a-minimum.md)), designed in
> [lifecycle.md](../design/lifecycle.md). **Full tier**: stored shapes (migrations 0085, 0086) and new
> routes; a pre-flight review (its findings folded in) and a final review. Ken asked for the run on
> 2026-10-13; decisions marked **(default)** are recommendations he may overturn.

**Goal:** whoever may `retire` deletes a component or a document as a tombstone, refused while
anything depends on it and saying what remains, and undeletes it; a restore naming something deleted
is refused, naming it; a tenant administrator declares a minimum keep per kind and per space, which
refuses deletion until it has passed (LIF-019, LIF-021, LIF-023, LIF-024, VER-023, VER-037, VER-044,
VER-047, VER-048).

| DL-B | **A tombstone** (ADR-0060): `artifact_deletion (id, artifact_id, act deleted/undeleted, by, at, written_by)`, insert-only and guarded as AH-C's archival; nothing is removed. `notDeletedAs(alias, snapshot?)` beside `notArchivedAs`, applied to every reader of the readable set: the listings, `readableComponents` (so baselines, publishing, numbering, comparison, live), the inbox, `changesSince`, attention, `streamScope`, `mayReference`; and `search_entry.deleted`, set by the act and by indexing. By id, `authorise`'s `targetOf` answers **410 `artifact_deleted`** to whoever may read it but for the tombstone (default), 404 to anyone else; Undelete and the view of what remains opt out. `recordVersion` re-checks it under the artifact's lock. Audited `artifact.deleted` and a new `artifact.undeleted`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| DL-C | **Refused** (LIF-023, LIF-021, VER-023, VER-037), every reason listed, not the first: a baseline pinning any version, superseded or not (`baseline_pin`, indexed by 0085); a publication placing any version (`publication_input`, indexed), or a request queued for one (`publication_request_occurrence`); a hold on it or its space (on hold, no reason); a document whose latest version references it, in any mode, deleted documents included (so undeleting one never revives a dangling reference); a lock held on it (`deletion.locked`, naming the holder) (default); an open review round on it (default); for a document, its publications, baselines and queued requests. Dependants the deleter may not read are neither named nor counted: the refusal says only that something they may not read depends on it (SCH-032; the residual stated in lifecycle.md). Cross-references, bindings, templates and themes name no deletable thing. **Consequence**: anything ever baselined or published is undeletable; archival is the way out. |
| DL-D | **Races**: one tenant-wide advisory lock, `alloy-works:deletion`, taken exclusive by delete and undelete and shared by every writer of a dependant (`mayReference`, a document's restore, `makeBaseline`, `requestPublication`, `placeHold`, retention writes).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| DL-E | **What remains** (LIF-024, VER-047, VER-048), stated in lifecycle.md and shown before deleting: its identity, versions and their content, uncut iterations, threads, suggestions, rounds and every audit event - unreachable but kept, read through the audit log and, by whoever may `retire` it, through the view of what remains; undeleting brings all back. Content goes only by erasure (ER). `GET /v1/changes` reports a deletion and an undeletion as changes (default).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| DL-F | **VER-044**: `mayReference` answers ok, refused, or deleted with the last title (to whoever may read it but for the tombstone); a document restore collects `reference.deleted` per node, the preview first. A component's restore references no component. An older document version and a comparison show a deleted reference as deleted, not as withheld.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| DL-G | **Retention** (DL2; LIF-019; cites VER-036, claimed by storage-and-versioning.md): a minimum keep in days per kind (component, document) and per space (default: kind, not component type), insert-only with its history, the latest row per key in force, by a tenant administrator, audited `retention.changed` (declared in 0085's rebuild). Deletion is refused until the longest keep has passed since the latest version. Undelete is never refused by a keep or a hold. audit.md's Retention section and `artifact.deleted`'s row updated. A new module (`keeps.ts`), not `retention.ts`, which is iterations'.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## Task 1: Deletion - DL1

- Migration 0085 (`artifact_deletion`, the indexes, the audit kinds); `packages/db/src/deletion.ts`;
  every reader gated; the routes; VER-044; Delete on the pages.
- Tests: each refusal, every reason listed, unreadable dependants neither named nor counted; a hold refusing; a racing
  dependant serialised; gone from every read, 410 to a reader, kept in the store and the log; undelete
  back; VER-044 naming a deleted component; only `retire`; audited.

## Task 2: Deleted, retention, and the close - DL2

- The Deleted listing and Undelete; migration 0086 (`retention_keep`); the routes; deletion refused
  within a keep; Administration > Retention.
- Tests: a keep per kind and per space, the longest applying; refused within it, allowed after; a hold
  refusing after a keep; undelete never refused; only a tenant administrator; axe in Light and Dark;
  the whole browser suite.
- The final review hunts a deletion escaping a dependant or a keep, a deleted artifact still read, and
  anything removed rather than kept. `features.md`, `README.md`, `architecture.md`, the CHANGELOG, the
  version (0.162.0) and the baseline.
