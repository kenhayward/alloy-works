# G5: The conversation

> T5's fifth slice ([generation.md](../design/generation.md), the order's G5): open prompts in a
> conversation, choosing an endpoint, a context tray, personal prompts, guardrails. **Full tier**: new
> tables, routes, a tool that creates. Ken's answers of 2026-10-18 are marked **(Ken)**; the rest
> **(default)**. The pre-flight review's findings are folded in.

**Goal:** a person holds a conversation with a model, on a document or on its own, putting their own
words to it under the assistant's governance; chooses the endpoint; sees and edits what it reads;
saves prompts of their own and promotes one deliberately; and a tenant's guardrails refuse, by name,
what it forbids in what is sent and what returns (GEN-047 to GEN-055).

## Decisions

| ID   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| G5-A | **A conversation is a sequence of runs** (Ken; GEN-047): `conversation` (owner, document or none, title, made), each turn a `generation_run` naming it. **A run keeps its transcript** (GN-I): 0126 adds `ask`, set at start, and `reply`, set once as it ends, verbatim, and `conversation_id`, all in `generation_run_kept`'s frozen set, its erasure carve-out kept. A turn sends the earlier finished turns' asks and replies, never their tool results, which the tray carries (G5-D); a failed or cancelled turn is kept on its run and replayed in none. One turn at a time (`turn_running`); at most 20 turns (`conversation_too_long`). Its owner's alone (GEN-016).                                                                          |
| G5-B | **An open prompt is governed as the assistant is** (GEN-048): the same instructions, tools as the requester, content as data, every write a proposal, every turn a run. No topic is refused for being unrelated (GEN-050); only a guardrail refuses (G5-F).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| G5-C | **Choosing an endpoint** (GEN-051): a turn names any endpoint in use with its key set, the chat route's by default, else drafting's; its name and boundary on every turn.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| G5-D | **The context tray** (Ken; GEN-053): `conversation_context`, the parts a conversation reads, added by a tool's read or by the person, removable, at most 50. Every turn re-reads each as the person, deciding `read` again (never trusted for having been shown): at the version placed for a document's conversation, else its latest; one no longer readable is left out, named to the person, absent from the call. Sent as data at the turn's start, recorded on the run's `context`. **Tools by kind**: on a document, G2's five; with none, `search` (the person's readable set), `read_component`, `propose_edit` at latest, and `propose_component` (G5-E).                                                                                    |
| G5-E | **Proposing a new component** (GEN-052): `propose_component { space, type, title, markdown }`, language and direction the document's or the tenant's default. 0128 lets a proposal create: `component_id` and `made_against` nullable, `creates`, an accepted creation setting its component once. Accepting needs `create` in the space, decided then, and makes it at 0.1 as the person; the generated report gains a third arm, an accepted creation, so GEN-025 and GN-H reach it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| G5-F | **Guardrails: declared phrases the service checks** (Ken; GEN-049): `guardrail` (a phrase, `sent`, `returned` or both, a reason), set by `administer` at the tenant and audited. Phrases alone, matched case-folded, NFC, spaces collapsed: no tenant pattern runs in the service. `sent` is checked over the ask, the tray and every tool result before each call; `returned` over the reply, which is held where any returned rule exists (the stream says it is checking) and sent once cleared. A match ends the turn `guardrail_refused`, naming the rule and reason, its proposals discarded. The floor (content as data, tools as the requester, writes as proposals) is code, not a rule; no route stores one that reaches tools or authority. |
| G5-G | **Personal prompts** (Ken; GEN-054, GEN-055): `personal_prompt` (owner, title, instructions), its owner's alone, never an artifact: a template binds artifact ids alone (TPL-061). Run as a turn's words. **Promote** copies it into a space the owner may edit as a new prompt at draft, its context, output and models declared then, review and a trial governing it; the row records what it became, audited. No other path. Out of the tenant export; erasing a person deletes their personal prompts, and leaves their conversations, with their trays (identities alone) and runs, unnamed.                                                                                                                                                     |
| G5-H | **GEN-021 is the model's** (default): the instructions say to name an instruction found in content and declined; a stand-in shows only that its words pass through. It is left for Ken's real-model run (GN-K), not cited by a test.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
