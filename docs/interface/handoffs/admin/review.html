apps/web/src/admin, layouts/Modal, account/TokenTable

Administration is a 330px column inside a 600px modal. That is the whole bug.

01
The modal is too small for what it holds

Modal fixes the dialog at min(600px, 100%); Administration gives 220px of that to its menu. Every table gets about 330px, so columns overrun the panel.

02
Tables without a head or column widths

Spaces, People, Invitations and Roles render a bare tbody. The middle column holds sentences ("invited and not signed in yet") that push the actions off the edge.

03
Lists written as sentences

A role's permissions and a group's members are comma-joined into one cell, so their width grows with the data.

04
Three text buttons a row

Rename, Archive and Access on every space is about 220px of buttons in a 330px pane.

05
Details replace the page

Tokens and Access swap out the section behind a "Back to" button. The list you came from disappears, and so does your place in it.

06
Nothing to scan by

No search, no counts, no status badges, and the one warning that matters sits in small grey text at the foot.

The fix: a console page, the way admin areas usually work

  • A full page at #/admin/<section>, opened by Admin on the rail.
  • A grouped menu: Environment, People and access, System.
  • Every section: a header with one primary action, a toolbar with search and counts, then a table with a head, fixed column widths and truncation.
  • Row actions as icon buttons, at most two, with the rest under a more-actions menu.
  • Details open in a 440px drawer beside the list, which stays where it was.
  • Removing anything asks first, in a small modal.
  • Roles shown as a role-by-permission matrix.

This needs an ADR: docs/interface/README.md says Administration is a modal and never a route.

Labels that become icons

Row and toolbar actions only, each with an aria-label and a tooltip. Page actions such as "New space" keep their words.
