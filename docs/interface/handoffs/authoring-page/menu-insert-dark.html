REGULATORY INFORMATION

Executive Summary. FDA grants the De Novo request for the Essilor® Stellest® spectacle lens and classifies prescription spectacle lenses to reduce the progression of myopia as a Class II device under new regulation 21 CFR 886.5845 (product code QUR). The device is indicated for correction of myopia with or without astigmatism and for slowing myopia progression in children aged 6–12 years with spherical equivalent refraction of −0.75 D to −4.50 D and astigmatism up to 1.50 D. A pivotal 24-month randomized controlled trial (FIN-3101, n=159) demonstrated a 71% reduction in myopia progression (spherical equivalent refraction) and a 53% reduction in axial elongation compared to single-vision control lenses. The probable benefits of the device outweigh its probable risks, which can be mitigated by general controls and the special controls identified herein.

Body

FDA identifies this generic type of device as:

Definition

Prescription spectacle lenses to reduce the progression of myopia. Prescription spectacle lenses to reduce the progression of myopia consist of spectacle lenses with additional physical optical design lens elements. In addition to optical correction of myopic refractive error, these lenses are intended to be used by patients who have myopia to reduce the rate of myopia progression. The lenses are mounted within a spectacle frame classified under § 886.5842.
