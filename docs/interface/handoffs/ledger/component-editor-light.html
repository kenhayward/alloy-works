Torque values for the pump housing

Reference v0.7 Editing, held by you Changes not in a version
List

Tighten the housing bolts in the order shown in Figure 2, in two passes. Use a calibrated torque wrench; a seating pass at half the final value prevents the gasket from creeping.1

  1. Fit the gasket dry. Do not use sealant on the mating face.
  2. Run every bolt down to 40 Nm in the sequence shown.
  3. Repeat the sequence at 85 Nm, then check each bolt once more.
    1. Hold the wrench square to the head.
    2. Where a bolt turns further, stop and read Fault codes 100 to 199.
Seating pass
A first pass at half the final torque, in sequence.
Final torque
85 Nm, measured on a clean, dry thread.

Where the housing is marked HT-400, the values above do not apply. Voir la notice du fabricant.fr

1 The seating pass is the manufacturer's recommendation for model 400 housings only.
