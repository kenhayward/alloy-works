Installation guide 2.0Acme Pumps

2 Installation

2.1 Before you begin

Read this section completely before unpacking the pump. The model 400 must be installed by a service engineer trained on the 400 series, on a level base that can carry at least twice the filled weight given in Table 2.1.

Keep the pump in its packing until the base is ready. Do not lift it by the motor housing or the inlet pipe.

Table 2.1 Weights and clearances
ItemValueNote
Weight, empty38 kgWith motor fitted
Weight, filled46 kgCoolant to the upper mark
Clearance, front600 mmFor filter access
Clearance, sides150 mmFor airflow

2.2 Install the coolant pump

  1. Set the pump on the base with the inlet facing the reservoir.
  2. Fit the four M10 anchor bolts and tighten them to 45 Nm.
  3. Connect the inlet and outlet hoses, then the supply cable.
